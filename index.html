WHAT YOU CAN REQUEST

Every certification and clearance issued by the barangay, now available through one online request form.

01
Barangay Certification / Clearance

General-purpose proof of residency for employment, school, or transactions.

02
Senior Citizen / PWD / Solo Parent Certification

Certification supporting benefits and discounts for qualified residents.

03
Certificate of Indigency

For residents applying for financial, medical, or educational assistance.

04
Certificate to File Action (CFA)

Required before filing a case in court, following barangay conciliation.

05
Certificate to Bar Action (CBA)

Confirms a dispute has gone through the barangay's conciliation process.

06
Certificate to Bar Counter-Claim (CBC)

Issued for counter-claims arising from barangay-level disputes.

07
Barangay Protection Order (BPO)

Emergency protection request for victims of abuse within the barangay.

08
Facilities & Properties Request

Reserve barangay halls, courts, or equipment for community use.
